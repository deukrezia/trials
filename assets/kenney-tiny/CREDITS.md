# Tiny Dungeon

Artwork by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), licensed under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
The original supplied license is retained in `LICENSE.txt`.

132 16 × 16 tiles use unmodified regions of `tilemap.png`, with 1px spacing and 12 columns, as specified by the supplied `Tiled/sampleSheet.tsx`.
The original atlas was copied from the user-provided `kenney_scribble-dungeons/Tilemap` directory. No AI-generated artwork is used.
